# 12 — Glossary (from the Crown base guide)

Reference context for the extractor, reviewer, and anyone reading decision records.
Source: `crown_letters/crown_macro_base_guide.pdf`.

## Letter sections
- **What's New This Week** — calendar of data, earnings, and policy events for the next
  five sessions; each tagged with what it hits and which open trades it affects.
- **Tactical Summary** — the trade table (the dashboard): every open and conditional
  position and its levels.
- **Positioning This Week** — what large speculators, dealers, and institutions are doing.
- **Notable Flow** — specific option blocks / institutional orders and Crown's read.
- **Featured Trade(s)** — one or two setups in depth: thesis, structure, strikes,
  invalidation, timing.
- **Previous Week Trade Review** — honest accounting of last week's trades.
- **Portfolio House View** — long-term posture across equities, gold, oil, rates, credit,
  dollar. Slower-moving than the tactical book.

## Tactical Summary columns
- **Market** — instrument + trade type. "META bear call" = bearish call spread on META.
  "Copper HG / CPER" = futures or ETF, depending on access.
- **View** — bullish, bearish, breakdown hedge, or pair trade.
- **Entry / Trigger** — the level entered at, or the condition to activate.
  **"Not triggered" = the market hasn't given the price yet. Do not chase.**
- **Stop / Risk** — where the thesis is wrong. Stocks/futures: a price level. Options
  spreads: a dollar loss or a behavioral rule ("close at 50% of max loss if SI sustains
  above $88 for 2 sessions").
- **Target** — staged T1/T2/T3; partial off at T1, rest runs.
- **Sizing** — multiplier of a standard position.

## Sizing
- Multiplier of *your* standard position: 1.00 full, 0.75, 0.50, 0.25. Not a % of portfolio.
- Tactical sleeve ≤ 10–20% of investable assets; a 1.00 position risks ≤ 1–2% of the
  sleeve — *risked*, not deployed.
- Work backward from the stop: risk = size × distance to stop. Options spreads: max loss
  is the risk.
- A 1.00 trade should hurt exactly twice as much as a 0.50 trade at the stop.

## Conviction (0–5 dots, independent of sizing)
- ●●●●● highest — setup, positioning, timing align; may add on confirmation.
- ●●●●○ strong — clean thesis, one variable unresolved. Most featured trades.
- ●●●○○ bias, **not actionable on its own**; often "no trade."
- ●●○○○ watching, not trading.
- ●○○○○ noted for the record; too early or too crowded.

## Positioning and market-structure terms
- **COT** — CFTC Commitments of Traders: positioning of large speculators, commercials,
  small traders in futures. "COT thesis" = trade built on that positioning.
- **Managed money** — hedge funds, CTAs, large specs; when crowded one way, fade the extreme.
- **Net long / net short** — long minus short contracts, often as % of open interest.
- **Open interest (OI)** — outstanding contracts; position vs OI = crowding.
- **Percentile** — reading vs its own last-52-week history.
- **IV / HV / IV30 / HV20** — implied vs realized vol; IV > HV = options rich.
- **Skew** — IV difference between OTM calls and puts; Crown trades against structurally
  steep skew.
- **Gamma (positive / negative)** — dealer hedging regime: positive compresses range,
  negative expands it.
- **Call wall / put wall** — largest dealer call/put exposure; call wall = magnet/ceiling,
  put wall = danger line below.
- **Zero gamma flip** — level where dealer gamma flips; above = stable, below = moves
  accelerate.
- **CTA flows** — systematic trend followers buying strength / selling weakness.
- **Notional** — dollar exposure, not cash posted.
- **Squeeze** — forced covering of a crowded short; fuel = high short interest.

## Option structures
- **Bear call spread (credit)** — sell lower call, buy higher call; profit if below the
  short strike at expiry. Max loss = width − credit.
- **Bull put spread (credit)** — mirror image; profit if above the short strike.
- **Seagull** — three legs; defined risk/reward across a band.
- **Pair trade** — long one, short another; profits from relative performance.
- **Credit vs debit** — credit pays you to enter (want nothing to happen); debit costs you
  (want the move).

## Operating principles (encode, don't paraphrase)
1. **We do not chase entries.** If a trigger hasn't hit, the trade does not exist.
2. **Check price is still near the entry** before acting; if it ran well past the
   trigger, it isn't the same trade. Stops/targets assume entry near published levels.
3. **Size for survival.** A 1.00 at the stop must not put you in trouble.
4. **Stops are not suggestions.** Raised as a trade works; never lowered.
5. **Take profit in stages.** Half off at T1 by default; rest runs with a raised stop.
6. **House view and tactical book are separate.** A tactical trade may conflict with the
   house view; that is not a reason to veto it.
7. **The letter is a read at the moment it was written.** Markets move between issues;
   mid-cycle updates follow the same workflow.
8. **Around scheduled events** (CPI, FOMC, major earnings), re-read the relevant sections
   the morning of the event.
